Small business lending collection and reporting requirements

Resources to help industry understand and comply with the small business lending rule, which implements section 1071 of the Dodd-Frank Act.

Featured topic

On May 1, 2026, the CFPB published a final rule to reconsider portions of the small business lending rule and extend its compliance date to January 1, 2028.  You can read the final rule here. The CFPB will update its regulatory and implementation materials to reflect the final rule at a later date.

Rule

Browse the small business lending final rule.

Regulations and official interpretations

Browse 12 CFR 1002 on: Interactive Bureau Regulations | eCFR

Main small business lending rule provisions and official interpretations can be found in:

  • § 1002.102, Definitions
  • § 1002.103 - .106, Coverage
  • § 1002.107, Data points collected
  • § 1002.108, Firewall
  • § 1002.109, Reporting requirements
  • Supplement I to Part 1002 (including official interpretations for the above provisions)
