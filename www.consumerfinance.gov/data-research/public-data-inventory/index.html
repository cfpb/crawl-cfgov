Explore the data the CFPB collects and publishes about the consumer financial marketplace.

Questions about data? Email CFPB_OCDO@cfpb.gov

Browse the FOIA reading room

Auto Loan Credit Trends

This dashboard provides access to car loan origination, inquiry, risk profile, income level and age profile data.

 Raw data Ongoing

CFPB Federal Open Data Inventory

CFPB priority datasets published in the Federal Data Catalog (Data.gov).

 Raw data Ongoing

CFPB Newsroom

Search engine for CFPB publications, reports, press releases, and other bureau-specific information.

 Various Ongoing

CFPB Web Traffic Analytics

This site provides an overview of how consumers are interacting with the CFPB’s webpages. The data comes from a unified Google Analytics account for U.S. federal government agencies, known as the Digital Analytics Program. Note: The program does not track individuals and anonymizes the IP addresses of visitors.

 Other Ongoing

College Credit Card Agreements

Information about college credit card agreements collected annually pursuant to the CARD Act from credit card issuers who have marketing agreements with universities, colleges, or affiliated organizations such as alumni associations, sororities, fraternities, and foundations.

 Documents Ongoing

College Banking Agreements and Related Data

Information about college banking partnerships from institutions of higher education (IHEs) that partner with financial service providers, including information about the number of student accountholders at specific IHEs, annual mean and median costs at specific IHEs, sample contracts between IHEs and financial service providers, and sample partnership disclosures provided by IHEs.

 Documents, Raw data One-time

Consumer Complaint Database

Consumer complaints about companies offering consumer financial products and services, including date of submission, consumer-selected issue, and company resolution categories. Also includes ZIP code and self-identified characteristics (e.g., older consumers, servicemember status).”

 Documents, Raw data Ongoing

Consumer Credit Card Market Report and Data

Aggregated data from multiple sources supporting figures in the CFPB’s biennial report to Congress on the consumer credit card market, as mandated by the CARD Act.

 Documents, Raw data Biennial

Consumer Reporting Agencies

This list includes the three nationwide consumer reporting companies as well as other companies that focus on certain market areas and consumer segments.

 Documents, Raw Data Annual

Costs of Electronic Payments in K-12 Schools

List of electronic payment processors and associated fees used by K-12 schools in 2023-2024. Data was collected from public sources, including school websites.

 Raw Data One-time

Credit Card Agreements

Credit card agreements from more than 300 card issuers. These agreements include general terms and conditions, pricing, and fee information and are collected pursuant to requirements in the CARD Act.

 Documents Ongoing

Interagency Data Inventory

 Metadata Annual

Mortgage Credit Trends

This dashboard provides access to mortgage origination, inquiry, risk profile, income level and age profile data.

 Raw data Ongoing

Mortgage Performance Trends

Data on mortgage delinquency rates from the National Mortgage Database (NMDB), which is jointly managed by the CFPB and Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA); data and analyses provided herein are the sole product of the CFPB.

 Raw data Quarterly

National Age-Friendly Banking Survey

The National Age Friendly Banking Survey (NAFBS) is a nationally representative survey of adults (ages 18 and older) that have bank or credit union accounts. The survey provides insights into consumers’ experiences with financial institutions, by allowing for comparisons across age groups.

 Documents, Raw data One-time

National Financial Well-Being Survey

A one-time nationally representative survey on financial well-being and related topics conducted by the CFPB in 2016. The purpose of the survey was to aid in the production of multiple reports on the existing state of financial well-being, as well as the relative contribution of various factors on individual’s financial well-being.

 Raw data One-time

National Survey of Mortgage Originations

Quarterly voluntary survey of a representative sample of recent mortgage borrowers about their experiences in choosing and taking out a mortgage. This is a joint collection between the CFPB and the FHFA.

 Documents, Raw data Quarterly

Payments to Harmed Consumers

This list includes payments made to compensate consumers harmed by a person or company that violated a consumer protection law.

 Documents Ongoing

Prepaid Products Agreement Database

Prepaid account agreement data, which contain general terms and conditions, pricing, and fee information, that issuers submit to the CFPB under the terms of the Prepaid Rule.

 Documents Daily

Reginfo.gov Information Collections

Lists collections of information from the public for which the CFPB has received prior approval from OMB, or approval is under review, as required by the Paperwork Reduction Act. Please filter on Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.

 Other Daily

Research Hub

CFPB’s PhD social scientists design and conduct foundational, policy-relevant research on consumer finance and household behavior. This is a repository of the CFPB’s research on consumer financial markets.

 Various Ongoing

Student Loan Credit Trends

This dashboard provides access to student loan origination, risk profile, income level and age profile data.

 Raw data Ongoing

Terms of Credit Card Plans (TCCP)

Semi-annual survey on the terms of credit card plans offered by over 150 financial institutions. Twice per year, the CFPB is required by law to collect certain credit card price and availability information from a sample of credit card issuers and report this information to Congress and the public.

 Raw data Twice annually

Tuition Payment Plans in Higher Education

Lists tuition payment plans for institutions of higher education (IHE). Data was collected from public sources, including college websites.

 Raw Data One-time
