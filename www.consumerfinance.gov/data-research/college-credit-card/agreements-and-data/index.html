College credit card agreements and data

This page provides agreements and related data about credit card issuers who have marketing agreements with universities, colleges, or affiliated organizations such as alumni associations, sororities, fraternities, and foundations.

The CFPB intends to ensure that the publicly available dataset is as accurate and complete as possible. This means that the dataset may not be consistent with past iterations of the data because the CFPB makes corrections to the combined dataset, as necessary. In all cases, the CFPB intends for the public dataset to be the CFPB’s definitive account of the data.
