Attorney Advisor

Division/Office:
Legal Division - Office of General Law & Oversight
Closing date:
Aug. 31, 2027
Location:
Washington, DC
Salary:
$132,205–255,000

Summary

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau’s Legal Division is seeking highly qualified attorneys with at least three years of experience to serve as Attorney Advisors in the Office of General Law & Oversight. The incumbent provides confidential legal/policy advice and representation primarily in either labor and employment or procurement law and may also provide advice in other general law matters. We are currently recruiting experienced attorneys at the CN-53 to CN-71 grade levels (equivalent to GS-13 to GS-15).

Labor and Employment Duties and Responsibilities Include:

  • Provide confidential legal and policy advice in the areas of labor and employment law, with an emphasis on labor negotiations and associated work.
  • Provide legal representation in labor and employment matters including participating in labor negotiations and serving as or assisting the Bureau's representative in labor arbitrations and litigation in administrative forums such as the EEOC and the MSPB, and Federal Courts.
  • Draft and respond to motions, pleadings, and other legal filings, handle all aspects of discovery, present oral argument, examine and cross-examine witnesses, and conduct negotiations in labor and employment matters.

Procurement Duties and Responsibilities Include:

  • Provide legal advice on procurement matters including defending the agency in contract protests before the GAO or the Court of Federal Claims.
  • Review solicitation and contract packages for compliance with federal procurement laws, regulations, policies, and procedures.
  • Provide advice to Contracting Officers on contract claims and disputes and represent the Bureau before the Civilian Board of Contract Appeals.

General Duties and Responsibilities Include:

  • Research and analyze relevant Federal and state laws, regulations, Executive Orders, and other government-wide directives and memoranda.
  • Counsels clients, including senior Bureau staff, with respect to legal compliance, litigation risk, and other issues.
  • Plan, research, and analyze legal issues arising from policy options and CFPB procedures.
  • Develop tactical and strategic plans for proposed actions and agency activity

If you are interested in being considered for this opportunity, please email a current resume to CFPB_LD_Careers@cfpb.gov and include "Application for GLO Attorney" in the subject line. This opportunity will remain open until the position is filled. If you are selected for this position, you will be required to provide transcripts from an accredited law school showing a law degree was conferred and proof that you are a member in good standing of a bar of any jurisdiction of the United States.

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau is a 21st century agency that implements and enforces Federal consumer financial law and ensures that markets for consumer financial products are transparent, fair, and competitive. Our goal is to recruit, hire, and retain the highest quality workforce based on merit, and to create a workplace that supports and develops talented individuals who want to do meaningful work on behalf of the Bureau.
