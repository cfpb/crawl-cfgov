Financial reports

Each fiscal year, the Chief Financial Officer releases a report on the Bureau’s finances. This report analyzes how funds were utilized and how we’ve done at meeting our stated goals for the year.

The annual Financial Report includes audited financial statements and the results of the financial audit. Between annual reports, we also publish quarterly updates on the current state of the Bureau’s financial resources.
